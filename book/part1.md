# MINECRAFT OVERTAKEN

## A Missionary's Guide to Reclaiming the Blocky World

*Fifteen missions. Three tiers of difficulty. One world to take back.*

---

> *"The Old Builders are gone. Their farms went wild, their lanterns died,
> and the night crept in where light once stood. The world has been
> overtaken — by shadow, by chaos, by the creatures that own the dark.
> But they left one thing behind: a map, a compass, and a commission.
> You are the missionary of the block. Your task is not merely to survive.
> Your task is to take the world back — one mission at a time."*
>
> — from the Book of the First Light

## HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

This is not a story you read. It is a mission log you complete.

The book contains **fifteen missions**, organized in three tiers of
difficulty:

| Tier | Missions | Difficulty | What you will do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier I | 1–5 | **Easy** | Build your first home, farm, road, tower, and mine |
| Tier II | 6–10 | **Normal** | Carve an underground base, smelt iron, bridge a chasm, enter the Nether, master redstone |
| Tier III | 11–15 | **Hard** | Hunt the stronghold, slay the dragon, raid the ocean monument, build a sky city, raise the Beacon of Overtaking |

Every mission follows the same format:

- **MISSION BRIEFING** — what is happening in the world, and why it matters
- **OBJECTIVE** — exactly what you must accomplish
- **SUPPLIES** — the blocks and items you must gather, and how many
- **COORDINATES** — exactly where in the world the mission takes place
- **THE PLAN** — the step-by-step execution
- **SUCCESS CHECKLIST** — how you will know the mission is complete
- **MISSIONARY NOTE** — a short reflection from the field

### Setting Up the World

Use these settings so the coordinates in this book match your world:

- **World name:** Overtaken
- **Seed:** `OVERTAKEN`
- **Game mode:** Survival
- **Difficulty:** Normal (Easy if you are new, Hard if you are a veteran)
- **World type:** Default
- **Cheats:** Off. A missionary earns every block.

### The Rules of the Mission Field

1. No creative mode. If a mission says collect 64 iron ore, you mine it.
2. No teleporting. You walk, you climb, you swim — or you build the thing
   that carries you.
3. If you die, you return and finish. The world does not reset for you.
4. A mission is only complete when every item on the success checklist is
   checked.

## READING THE COORDINATES

Every mission in this book gives you exact coordinates. Before you take a
single step, learn to read them. This is the single most important skill
in the book.

**The three numbers are always in the same order: X, Y, Z.**

| Axis | Meaning | Positive (+) | Negative (−) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X** | East / West | East | West |
| **Y** | Up / Down (height) | Up | Down |
| **Z** | South / North | South | North |

**Sea level is Y = 63.** This is your anchor. If a mission says Y = 64, you
are one block above sea level — standing on the surface. If it says Y = 16,
you are 47 blocks underground, near the diamond layer. If it says Y = 150,
you are building in the sky.

Here is how to read a pair of camps from an old mission log:

| Camp | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Camp 1 | −185 | 61 | −190 |
| Camp 2 | −189 | 5 | −161 |

Reading them:

- Camp 2 is **4 blocks west** of Camp 1 (X went −185 → −189, toward negative = west)
- Camp 2 is **56 blocks lower** (Y went 61 → 5 — that is a deep tunnel; Camp 1 sits just below the surface, Camp 2 crouches just above bedrock)
- Camp 2 is **29 blocks north** of Camp 1 (Z went −190 → −161, toward positive = south... wait — −161 is *more* positive than −190, so Camp 2 is actually **29 blocks south**)

**Rule of thumb:**

- **X or Z changes → you are moving sideways** (north / south / east / west)
- **Y changes → you are moving up or down**
- Y is your floor number. To find a place again: match X and Z first, then climb or dig until Y matches too.

**The one exception:** the Nether. Coordinates there are roughly one-eighth
of the Overworld. This book gives you both sets when a mission crosses over.

## MISSION 1 — THE FIRST FOUNDATION

**Difficulty: Easy**

### Mission Briefing

You stand at the world's heart — spawn, at (0, 63, 0). The grass is tall,
the trees are wild, and nothing here belongs to anyone. The Old Builders
left no shelter. Before you can reclaim anything, you must have a place
to be safe when the sun goes down. A missionary without a home is a
missionary who does not last the night.

### Objective

Build a **7×7 wooden house** at the mission plot, with a door, a bed, four
glass windows, and twelve torches. Sleep in it before dawn.

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Oak logs | 64 |
| Oak planks | 160 |
| Cobblestone | 96 |
| Glass | 16 |
| Wooden door | 1 |
| Bed | 1 |
| Torches | 12 |

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plot corner (southwest) | 24 | 64 | 12 |
| Plot corner (northeast) | 30 | 64 | 18 |
| Front wall | 24 | 64–67 | 12–18 |
| Door position | 26 | 64 | 12 |
| Roof level | 24–30 | 68 | 12–18 |

The house occupies X 24–30 and Z 12–18 — a 7×7 footprint with a 5×5
interior. Walls rise from Y 64 to Y 67. The roof caps it at Y 68.

### The Plan

1. **Gather the wood.** Fell oak trees until you have 64 logs. Turn them
   into planks: 64 logs = 256 planks. You need 160 for the build and 96
   for crafting tools and torches.
2. **Mark the plot.** Stand at (24, 64, 12). Lay a row of cobblestone
   along Z 12 from X 24 to X 30. This is your front wall's foundation.
3. **Raise the walls.** Build the front wall at Z 12 from Y 64 to Y 67.
   Leave a two-block gap at (26–27, 64, 12) for the door. Build the back
   wall at Z 18, and the side walls at X 24 and X 30.
4. **Add the windows.** Place glass at (25, 66, 12), (27, 66, 12),
   (29, 66, 12) on the front, and one more on the back wall at
   (27, 66, 18). Four windows, four directions of light.
5. **Roof it.** Cap the top at Y 68 with oak planks, all the way across.
6. **Furnish.** Place the door in the gap. Put the bed against the back
   wall at (28, 64, 17). Craft and place a crafting table and a furnace.
7. **Light it.** Place torches: one in each interior corner at Y 65, two
   on the front wall outside, two on the back, and four around the roof
   edge. Twelve torches total.
8. **Sleep.** Right-click the bed as night falls. This sets your spawn
   point — the first claim in your new territory.

### Success Checklist

- [ ] Walls complete from Y 64 to Y 67 with no gaps
- [ ] Door closes and opens
- [ ] Four glass windows placed
- [ ] Bed placed and slept in
- [ ] Twelve torches burning
- [ ] No hostile mobs inside the house at dawn

### Missionary Note

*A house is the first act of claiming. Before you can bring light to the
world, you need a place to keep your own. The Old Builders forgot this,
and their light went out. Build small, build solid, build light — then
move outward. The world is taken back one foundation at a time.*

## MISSION 2 — THE BREAD BASKET

**Difficulty: Easy**

### Mission Briefing

A missionary marches on bread. The wild grass around the camp carries
wheat seeds — the Old Builders' farms are gone, but their grain survives
in the tall grass, waiting to be gathered and planted again. This mission
turns wild seed into a working farm: the first food supply of the
reclamation.

### Objective

Build a **fenced 9×9 wheat farm** at the mission field, plant **32 wheat
seeds**, and harvest **32 wheat**. One water source must water the whole
field.

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Wheat seeds | 32 |
| Hoe (wooden or stone) | 1 |
| Water bucket | 1 |
| Fence | 28 |
| Fence gate | 1 |
| Torches | 8 |

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field corner (northwest) | −45 | 63 | 80 |
| Field corner (southeast) | −37 | 63 | 88 |
| Water channel | −41 | 63 | 84 |
| Fence perimeter | −46 to −36 | 63 | 79 to 89 |
| Gate | −45 | 63 | 84 |

The field spans X −45 to −37 and Z 80 to 88. A single water source at
(−41, 63, 84) sits in the middle, hydrating all 80 farmland blocks around
it.

### The Plan

1. **Collect the seeds.** Break tall grass until you have 32 wheat seeds.
   (You may find more — keep them; you will need them.)
2. **Till the field.** With the hoe, till every block in the 9×9 area
   except the center block at (−41, 63, 84).
3. **Water it.** Use the water bucket at the center. The water will flow
   one block in every direction, hydrating the entire field. Cover the
   source with a slab if you want to walk across without falling in.
4. **Plant.** Plant all 32 seeds in the tilled soil. If you have more
   seeds, plant them too — a full field is a richer harvest.
5. **Fence it.** Ring the field with fence: 28 pieces, one block outside
   the tilled edge. Place the fence gate on the west side at
   (−45, 63, 84) so the field stays open to you and closed to mobs.
6. **Light the edges.** Place 8 torches on the fence corners and midpoint
   posts so no mobs spawn on or near the field at night.
7. **Wait and harvest.** Wheat grows in stages. When it is golden and
   tall, break it and collect the wheat. You need 32 in your inventory
   to complete the mission — but replant the seeds you drop. A bread
   basket is a cycle, not a one-time harvest.

### Success Checklist

- [ ] 32 wheat harvested and in inventory
- [ ] Field fully fenced with a working gate
- [ ] One water source hydrating the field
- [ ] 8 torches placed around the perimeter
- [ ] Seeds replanted for the next harvest

### Missionary Note

*The world will not feed you because you ask. It feeds you because you
work. The Old Builders' fields were overtaken by grass the moment they
stopped tending them — that is the law of this world: whatever you do not
tend, the wild takes back. A farm is a promise you renew every day.*

## MISSION 3 — THE LANTERN ROAD

**Difficulty: Easy**

### Mission Briefing

The way from spawn to the eastern ridge is dark, and in the dark the
world's creatures hunt. The Old Builders believed roads were for carts.
The missionary knows better: roads are for light. A lit road is a line
drawn through the night, a claim staked in torchlight from horizon to
horizon.

### Objective

Light a **torch path** from spawn (0, 63, 0) to the eastern ridge at
(100, 64, 100), placing **32 torches** along the route. Then build a
**10-block-high torch tower** at the ridge and light its crown.

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Coal | 48 |
| Sticks | 48 |
| Cobblestone | 64 |
| Stone slabs | 32 |

*(48 coal + 48 sticks = 48 torches. You will place 32 on the road, keep
the rest for the tower and spares.)*

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Path start | 0 | 63 | 0 |
| Path end (ridge) | 100 | 64 | 100 |
| Torch spacing | every 3 blocks along the diagonal | — | — |
| Tower base | 100 | 64 | 100 |
| Tower crown | 100 | 74 | 100 |

The path runs diagonally northeast: X and Z rise together, 0 → 100. Place
a torch every 4 blocks: (4, 63, 4), (8, 63, 8), (12, 63, 12), and so on
until (100, 64, 100).

### The Plan

1. **Gather fuel and light.** Mine 48 coal. Craft 48 sticks, then 48
   torches. (You will need more later in the book — this is your first
   torch run; it will not be your last.)
2. **Pave the stepping stones.** Lay cobblestone and slabs in short
   stretches along the diagonal so the route is walkable. You do not
   need a full road — a missionary builds for light, not for carts.
3. **Plant the torches.** Starting at (3, 63, 3), place a torch roughly
   every three blocks along the diagonal — about 32 torches over the
   run. Where the ground rises, put the torch on a cobblestone stub so
   it never sits below your eye line.
4. **Reach the ridge.** The path ends at (100, 64, 100). From here you
   can see back to spawn — which is exactly the point.
5. **Build the tower.** At (100, 64, 100), stack cobblestone to Y 74.
   Place a torch on top of the crown and four more around the sides at
   Y 73. This is the Lantern of the East: the first landmark of the
   reclamation.
6. **Check the gaps.** Walk the path at night. No stretch of darkness may
   exceed 8 blocks. Add torches where the light pools thin.

### Success Checklist

- [ ] 32 torches placed along the path at roughly 4-block intervals
- [ ] Path visible end to end at night from spawn
- [ ] Tower at (100, 64, 100) standing 10 blocks high
- [ ] Tower crown lit with 5 torches
- [ ] No dark gap longer than 8 blocks anywhere on the route

### Missionary Note

*Light is the first weapon of the missionary. Mobs do not respect your
sword until they fear your torches. Every torch you plant is a small
sermon: this ground is claimed, this ground is watched, this ground
belongs to the light again.*

## MISSION 4 — THE WATCHTOWER

**Difficulty: Easy**

### Mission Briefing

To the northwest, the forest opens into a plain that the Old Builders
called the Quiet Field. It is not quiet anymore — it is overtaken. From
the rise at (−120, −40) you can see the whole plain, and something else:
the first true test of a builder. A tower that can see is a tower that
can warn. The reclamation needs eyes.

### Objective

Build a **12-block-high watchtower** at the mission site, with a working
ladder, a fenced observation platform, and **16 torches**. From the top,
you must be able to see the Lantern of the East at (100, 64, 100).

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cobblestone | 192 |
| Oak planks | 96 |
| Ladders | 24 |
| Fence | 16 |
| Glass | 8 |
| Torches | 16 |

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tower footprint (southwest corner) | −120 | 64 | −40 |
| Tower footprint (northeast corner) | −114 | 64 | −34 |
| Base height | −120 to −114 | 64–65 | −40 to −34 |
| Wall tops | −120 to −114 | 76 | −40 to −34 |
| Observation platform | −119 to −115 | 76 | −39 to −35 |
| Ladder (north face) | −117 | 64–75 | −40 |
| Windows | −120 | 70 | −37, and −117 | 70 | −40 |

The tower is a 7×7 hollow shaft. Its walls rise from Y 64 to Y 76 —
12 blocks of stone against the sky.

### The Plan

1. **Gather stone.** Mine 192 cobblestone. A nearby hill should cover
   most of it.
2. **Lay the base.** Build the 7×7 footprint two blocks high (Y 64–65).
   Leave the inside hollow.
3. **Raise the shaft.** From the base, raise the four walls to Y 76 —
   ten more blocks. Leave the ladder shaft open on the north face at
   (−117, Z −40).
4. **Windows.** At Y 70, place glass at (−120, 70, −37) on the west wall
   and (−117, 70, −40) on the north wall. Small windows, watchful eyes.
5. **The ladder.** Run ladders up the north face shaft from Y 64 to
   Y 75 — 24 ladders, no gaps. Test the climb.
6. **The platform.** At Y 76, floor the top with oak planks. Ring it
   with fence. Place torches in the four corners and along the fence —
   16 torches total on the tower.
7. **Look east.** From the platform, face (100, 64, 100). If the night
   is clear, the Lantern of the East answers your light with its own.

### Success Checklist

- [ ] Tower stands 12 blocks from base to platform
- [ ] Ladder climbs the full height without gaps
- [ ] Platform floored and fully fenced
- [ ] 16 torches placed
- [ ] Lantern of the East visible from the top

### Missionary Note

*The Watchtower teaches the second law of the mission field: see before
you are seen. The creatures of the overtaken world ambush what they can
surprise. A tower does not fight for you — it sees for you, and seeing
is half the battle. Raise your eyes, and the night loses its advantage.*

## MISSION 5 — THE FIRST DESCENT

**Difficulty: Easy**

### Mission Briefing

The surface can feed you and shelter you, but it cannot arm you. Iron
waits below — and below is where the world keeps its oldest secrets and
its darkest rooms. Every missionary must learn to go down. This mission
teaches the descent: a proper shaft, a proper ladder, a proper light
discipline, and the first real ore of the reclamation.

### Objective

Dig a **1×2 vertical shaft** at the mission site down to **Y 16**, then
branch-mine the tunnel at Y 16 and collect **32 iron ore**.

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Stone pickaxes | 3 |
| Ladders | 40 |
| Torches | 32 |
| Oak planks | 32 |
| Cooked food | 16 |

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shaft mouth | 50 | 63 | −70 |
| Shaft bottom | 50 | 16 | −70 |
| Branch tunnel | 50 → 70 | 16 | −70 |
| Side branches | 50 → 70 | 16 | −72 and −68 |

The shaft drops 47 blocks straight down from (50, 63, −70) to
(50, 16, −70). At the bottom, a branch tunnel runs east from X 50 to
X 70 at Y 16, with side branches at Z −72 and Z −68.

### The Plan

1. **Prepare.** Craft 3 stone pickaxes (48 cobblestone + 3 sticks × 3),
   40 ladders, and 32 torches. Eat before you descend — mining is hungry
   work.
2. **Dig the shaft.** At (50, 63, −70), dig a 1×2 hole straight down.
   Place ladders on one side of the shaft as you go, every block, no
   gaps. Torch the shaft every 8 blocks as you descend.
3. **Follow the floor.** When your Y readout says 16, stop. You are at
   the mission floor — deep enough for iron in abundance, safe from lava
   lakes above the diamond layer.
4. **Branch-mine.** Dig the main tunnel east from (50, 16, −70) to
   (70, 16, −70). Then dig side branches two blocks apart at Z −72 and
   Z −68, 20 blocks long each. This pattern exposes the most ore for the
   least digging.
5. **Collect the iron.** Mine every iron ore block you find with your
   stone pickaxe. Do not smelt yet — this mission is about gathering.
   You need 32 blocks of iron ore in your inventory.
6. **Light as you go.** Torch every tunnel every 8 blocks. A dark tunnel
   is a mob nursery. You are a missionary of light — even underground.
7. **Come back up.** Climb the ladder. Check your Y as you rise: 20, 30,
   40, 50, 60 — the world's floors rolling past like stairs.

### Success Checklist

- [ ] Shaft bottom reached at Y 16
- [ ] Ladders placed the full 47-block descent
- [ ] Branch tunnels dug to spec
- [ ] 32 iron ore in inventory
- [ ] Every tunnel lit, no spawns in the shaft

### Missionary Note

*The descent is the third lesson: the world's wealth is buried, and so
is its danger. The Old Builders mined greedily and left their shafts
dark; the creatures of the deep moved in behind them. When you go down,
light everything and leave nothing behind. A clean mine is a claim that
stays claimed.*

## MISSION 6 — THE UNDERGROUND STRONGROOM

**Difficulty: Normal**

### Mission Briefing

The surface is safe now — your house stands, your farm feeds you, your
tower watches. But a missionary's treasure should not sit where every
wandering eye can see it. Two hundred blocks east and three hundred
south, a hill hides a secret: the perfect place for a strongroom.
You will cut a staircase into the hill and carve a home out of the
earth's bones.

### Objective

Cut a **staircase tunnel** from the surface at (200, 63, 300) down to
**(200, 30, 300)**, then carve a **15×15×4 room** around that point and
furnish it with 16 chests, 8 furnaces, 4 crafting tables, and a bed.

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Torches | 64 |
| Chests | 16 |
| Furnaces | 8 |
| Crafting tables | 4 |
| Ladders | 64 |
| Doors | 2 |
| Bed | 1 |
| Iron ingots | 32 |

*(The iron ingots are for the door hinges and the furnace upgrades — a
strongroom without iron is a pantry.)*

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tunnel mouth (surface) | 200 | 63 | 300 |
| Staircase | 200 | 63 → 30 | 300 |
| Room floor (southwest corner) | 193 | 30 | 293 |
| Room floor (northeast corner) | 207 | 30 | 307 |
| Ceiling | 193–207 | 34 | 293–307 |

The staircase drops 33 blocks straight down the Z axis at X 200. At
Y 30, the room opens: X 193–207, Z 293–307 — a 15×15 floor with a
4-block ceiling height.

### The Plan

1. **Mark the mouth.** At (200, 63, 300) on the hillside, dig a 2-wide
   entrance.
2. **Cut the staircase.** Dig down in a straight run, 2 blocks wide, with
   a step down every block. Torch both walls every 8 blocks. Check your
   Y as you go: 55, 45, 35 — and stop at 30.
3. **Carve the room.** At Y 30, expand in all directions to the corners
   (193, 30, 293) and (207, 30, 307). Cut the ceiling flat at Y 34.
4. **Seal and light.** Line the walls with torches on every pillar face —
   a 15×15 room needs about 40 torches to stay spawn-proof. Place the
   rest along the staircase.
5. **Furnish.** Chests against the north and south walls — 8 per wall,
   16 total. Furnaces along the east wall — 8 of them. Crafting tables
   in the four corners. Bed against the west wall.
6. **Doors.** Place a door at the tunnel mouth and a second at the room
   entrance. The strongroom is yours; the world stays outside.
7. **Inventory.** Fill the chests with everything you have gathered so
   far — iron ore from Mission 5, wheat from Mission 2, spare cobble and
   coal. A missionary who loses his stores loses his momentum.

### Success Checklist

- [ ] Staircase complete from Y 63 to Y 30, fully lit
- [ ] Room carved to exact 15×15×4 dimensions
- [ ] 16 chests placed
- [ ] 8 furnaces placed
- [ ] 4 crafting tables placed
- [ ] Bed placed; no mob spawns anywhere in the room

### Missionary Note

*The strongroom is the fourth lesson: what you gather, you must guard.
The Old Builders piled their wealth in the open and it was scattered in
a single night. Bury what you love, light what you bury, and the world
will have to work to take it from you.*

## MISSION 7 — THE IRONWORKS

**Difficulty: Normal**

### Mission Briefing

Your chests are full of iron ore, but ore is only a promise. Iron becomes
useful only when it passes through fire. Three hundred fifty blocks east
of spawn, an outcrop of stone at (350, 64, 100) is the natural site for
the Ironworks — a row of furnaces that will turn the promise of ore into
the currency of the reclamation: iron ingots.

### Objective

Build an **8-furnace smelting works** at the mission site with a
**hopper-fed chest system**, then smelt **64 iron ingots** and bank them
in the chest.

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Iron ore | 64 (mined, not smelted) |
| Cobblestone | 128 |
| Furnaces | 8 |
| Chests | 4 |
| Hoppers | 8 |
| Coal | 64 |
| Crafting table | 1 |

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Furnace row | 350 → 357 | 64 | 100 |
| Hopper row (below) | 350 → 357 | 63 | 100 |
| Collection chests | 352–353 | 62 | 102–103 |

Eight furnaces sit in a row along Z 100, X 350 through 357. Each furnace
feeds a hopper directly beneath it at Y 63; the hoppers feed chests at
(352–353, 62, 102–103).

### The Plan

1. **Feed the mission.** Mine 64 iron ore if your stores are short —
   follow the Mission 5 branch-mine pattern in fresh ground. You also
   need 64 coal: any coal seam at Y 16–40 will do.
2. **Build the works.** Place 8 furnaces in a row: (350, 64, 100)
   through (357, 64, 100).
3. **Plumb the hoppers.** Dig out the blocks beneath each furnace
   (Y 63) and place a hopper there, pointed down. Dig a small channel at
   Y 62 leading to the chest position at (352–353, 62, 102–103). Place
   the four chests.
4. **Wire the flow.** Each hopper must point toward the next until the
   line reaches the chests. Sneak-place the hoppers facing the correct
   direction — this is the fiddliest step, and the most rewarding.
5. **Fire the works.** Load each furnace with 8 coal and 8 iron ore.
   Light them. As each ingot smelts, the hopper below catches it and
   carries it to the chest.
6. **Bank the ingots.** Run the works until 64 iron ingots sit in the
   chest. Count them. When the count is right, the mission is done.

### Success Checklist

- [ ] 8 furnaces in a row at the mission coordinates
- [ ] 8 hoppers connected, all feeding the chest line
- [ ] 4 chests placed and reachable
- [ ] 64 iron ingots banked in the chest
- [ ] Works lit and safe to operate at night

### Missionary Note

*Ore is potential; ingot is power. The Ironworks teaches the fifth
lesson: build systems, not just things. The Old Builders smelted one
furnace at a time and spent their days tending fires. You will build a
machine that tends itself while you do the next mission. Systems are how
a single missionary reclaims a world.*

## MISSION 8 — THE CHASM BRIDGE

**Difficulty: Normal**

### Mission Briefing

Four hundred blocks east of spawn, the land tears open. A ravine — the
Old Builders called it the God-Split — runs for hundreds of blocks,
darker than a wound, deeper than a well. The eastern lands beyond it
hold the Nether gate site, but the ravine blocks the way. The creatures
below own the dark. The missionary's answer is not to go around. The
missionary's answer is to build across.

### Objective

Bridge the ravine from the **west lip at (400, 70, −200)** to the
**east lip at (470, 70, −180)**, with a 2-wide plank deck, fences on
both sides, and 16 torches. Cross it safely.

### Supplies

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Oak planks | 192 |
| Fence | 192 |
| Torches | 16 |
| Cobblestone | 64 |

### Coordinates

| Landmark | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| West lip | 400 | 70 | −200 |
| East lip | 470 | 70 | −180 |
| Deck | 400 → 470 | 70 | −200 → −180 |
| Torch spacing | every 8 blocks along the deck | — | — |

The ravine runs roughly southwest–northeast. The bridge crosses at
Y 70, 7 blocks above the surrounding ground (Y 63). The deck runs about
70 blocks from X 400 to X 470, drifting south as it goes (Z −200 to
−180).

### The Plan

1. **Scout both lips.** Walk the ravine edge at (400, 70, −200) and find
   the matching ledge at (470, 70, −180). Mark both with torches.
2. **Build the anchor.** At the west lip, lay a cobblestone pad 4×4 so

Take it immediately — the star is the only thing in this mission that outshines the Wither itself, and the shadow will send a final line of strays down the shaft to rob you of it. Kill them or outrun them. The star in your pack is the crown now.

10. **Return through the ash.** Climb the shaft, reach the portal at (−2200, 63, 1900), and step through to the basalt field at (−275, 70, 238). The bunker is southwest with the sun — walk the Nether by compass and torchlight until you stand on the bunker's obsidian floor. Step through the old gate and emerge at (500, 64, −400) as the second sunrise reddens the east. If the first light is already up, run west. The road from the gate to spawn is 500 blocks of open ground, and you still have 500 more to carry.

11. **Crown the Beacon.** At (0, 67, 0), climb the Beacon of Overtaking and set the Nether Star into the socket at its crown. The star seats itself with a sound like a bell struck underwater. The Beacon's light doubles; a second beam goes up, then a third, then a fourth, until the column of light is the shape of something that cannot be outshone. Stand on the crown and watch the sun come up again. The eclipse is broken. The shadow's crown is yours. The Wither is dead, and you are the one who buried it and wore its star.

## APPENDIX A — READING COORDINATES

The complete cheat sheet for every coordinate in this book.

**The order is always X, Y, Z.**

- **X:** east (+) or west (−)
- **Y:** up (+) or down (−) — your altitude
- **Z:** south (+) or north (−)

**Sea level is Y = 63.** Everything in this book measures from there.

**To find any mission site:** match X and Z first (that puts you in the
right horizontal place), then adjust Y (that puts you at the right
floor). X or Z changes mean sideways movement. Y changes mean up or
down.

**Nether rule:** Nether coordinates are roughly Overworld ÷ 8. The
portal at Overworld (500, −400) lands you at Nether (62, −50).

## APPENDIX D — GLOSSARY

- **Beacon** — a block that projects a beam of light into the sky when
  placed atop a mineral pyramid; grants powers to those in its glow.
- **Branch mining** — digging parallel tunnels two blocks apart to
  expose maximum ore for minimum effort.
- **Conduit** — a heart of prismarine and nautilus shell that grants
  water breathing and vision underwater when framed in prismarine.
- **Elder guardian** — the ancient, cursed fish that guard ocean
  monuments; they inflict Mining Fatigue.
- **End Crystal** — the healing crystal atop each obsidian pillar in
  the End; the dragon cannot die while they remain.
- **Eye of Ender** — crafted from ender pearl and blaze powder; flies
  toward the nearest stronghold when thrown.
- **Ender Dragon** — the tyrant of the End dimension, and the final
  boss of the mission field.
- **Ghast** — a large floating fire creature of the Nether that fires
  explosive fireballs.
- **God-Split** — the ravine crossed by the Chasm Bridge; a missionary
  landmark.
- **Hopper** — a block that moves items from one container to another;
  the heart of any automated system.
- **Lantern of the East** — the torch tower at (100, 64, 100), first
  landmark of the reclamation.
- **Nether** — the dimension of fire reached through a lit obsidian
  portal; coordinates are roughly one-eighth of the Overworld's.
- **Piglin** — a gold-loving creature of the Nether; neutral unless
  provoked.
- **Repeater** — a redstone component that refreshes and delays a
  signal, letting it travel farther than 15 blocks.
- **Silverfish** — a small mob that bursts from mined stone brick in
  strongholds; swarm with friends.
- **Spawn** — the point (0, 63, 0) where you first entered the world
  and where the reclamation began.
