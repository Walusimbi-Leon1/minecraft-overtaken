MINECRAFT OVERTAKEN

A Missionary’s Guide to Reclaiming the Blocky World

Fifteen missions. Three tiers of difficulty. One world to take back.

“The Old Builders are gone. Their farms went wild, their lanterns died, and the night crept in where light once stood. The world has been overtaken — by shadow, by chaos, by the creatures that own the dark. But they left one thing behind: a map, a compass, and a commission. You are the missionary of the block. Your task is not merely to survive. Your task is to take the world back — one mission at a time.”

— from the Book of the First Light

HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

This is not a story you read. It is a mission log you complete.

The book contains fifteen missions, organized in three tiers of difficulty:

Tier Missions Difficulty What you will do
Tier I 1–5 Easy Build your first home, farm, road, tower, and mine
Tier II 6–10 Normal Carve an underground base, smelt iron, bridge a chasm, enter the Nether, master redstone
Tier III 11–15 Hard Hunt the stronghold, slay the dragon, raid the ocean monument, build a sky city, raise the Beacon of Overtaking

Every mission follows the same format:

  • MISSION BRIEFING — what is happening in the world, and why it matters
  • OBJECTIVE — exactly what you must accomplish
  • SUPPLIES — the blocks and items you must gather, and how many
  • COORDINATES — exactly where in the world the mission takes place
  • THE PLAN — the step-by-step execution
  • SUCCESS CHECKLIST — how you will know the mission is complete
  • MISSIONARY NOTE — a short reflection from the field

Setting Up the World

Use these settings so the coordinates in this book match your world:

  • World name: Overtaken
  • Seed: OVERTAKEN
  • Game mode: Survival
  • Difficulty: Normal (Easy if you are new, Hard if you are a veteran)
  • World type: Default
  • Cheats: Off. A missionary earns every block.

The Rules of the Mission Field

  1. No creative mode. If a mission says collect 64 iron ore, you mine it.
  2. No teleporting. You walk, you climb, you swim — or you build the thing that carries you.
  3. If you die, you return and finish. The world does not reset for you.
  4. A mission is only complete when every item on the success checklist is checked.

READING THE COORDINATES

Every mission in this book gives you exact coordinates. Before you take a single step, learn to read them. This is the single most important skill in the book.

The three numbers are always in the same order: X, Y, Z.

Axis Meaning Positive (+) Negative (−)
X East / West East West
Y Up / Down (height) Up Down
Z South / North South North

Sea level is Y = 63. This is your anchor. If a mission says Y = 64, you are one block above sea level — standing on the surface. If it says Y = 16, you are 47 blocks underground, near the diamond layer. If it says Y = 150, you are building in the sky.

Here is how to read a pair of camps from an old mission log:

Camp X Y Z
Camp 1 −185 61 −190
Camp 2 −189 5 −161

Reading them:

  • Camp 2 is 4 blocks west of Camp 1 (X went −185 → −189, toward negative = west)
  • Camp 2 is 56 blocks lower (Y went 61 → 5 — that is a deep tunnel; Camp 1 sits just below the surface, Camp 2 crouches just above bedrock)
  • Camp 2 is 29 blocks north of Camp 1 (Z went −190 → −161, toward positive = south… wait — −161 is more positive than −190, so Camp 2 is actually 29 blocks south)

Rule of thumb:

  • X or Z changes → you are moving sideways (north / south / east / west)
  • Y changes → you are moving up or down
  • Y is your floor number. To find a place again: match X and Z first, then climb or dig until Y matches too.

The one exception: the Nether. Coordinates there are roughly one-eighth of the Overworld. This book gives you both sets when a mission crosses over.

TIER I — EASY

The first five missions stay near the surface. They teach you the rhythm of the missionary life: gather, build, light, plant, descend. Do not skip them. Even a veteran must learn the ground they stand on.

MISSION 1 — THE FIRST FOUNDATION

Difficulty: Easy

Mission Briefing

You stand at the world’s heart — spawn, at (0, 63, 0). The grass is tall, the trees are wild, and nothing here belongs to anyone. The Old Builders left no shelter. Before you can reclaim anything, you must have a place to be safe when the sun goes down. A missionary without a home is a missionary who does not last the night.

Objective

Build a 7×7 wooden house at the mission plot, with a door, a bed, four glass windows, and twelve torches. Sleep in it before dawn.

Supplies

Item Quantity
Oak logs 64
Oak planks 160
Cobblestone 96
Glass 16
Wooden door 1
Bed 1
Torches 12

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
Plot corner (southwest) 24 64 12
Plot corner (northeast) 30 64 18
Front wall 24 64–67 12–18
Door position 26 64 12
Roof level 24–30 68 12–18

The house occupies X 24–30 and Z 12–18 — a 7×7 footprint with a 5×5 interior. Walls rise from Y 64 to Y 67. The roof caps it at Y 68.

The Plan

  1. Gather the wood. Fell oak trees until you have 64 logs. Turn them into planks: 64 logs = 256 planks. You need 160 for the build and 96 for crafting tools and torches.
  2. Mark the plot. Stand at (24, 64, 12). Lay a row of cobblestone along Z 12 from X 24 to X 30. This is your front wall’s foundation.
  3. Raise the walls. Build the front wall at Z 12 from Y 64 to Y 67. Leave a two-block gap at (26–27, 64, 12) for the door. Build the back wall at Z 18, and the side walls at X 24 and X 30.
  4. Add the windows. Place glass at (25, 66, 12), (27, 66, 12), (29, 66, 12) on the front, and one more on the back wall at (27, 66, 18). Four windows, four directions of light.
  5. Roof it. Cap the top at Y 68 with oak planks, all the way across.
  6. Furnish. Place the door in the gap. Put the bed against the back wall at (28, 64, 17). Craft and place a crafting table and a furnace.
  7. Light it. Place torches: one in each interior corner at Y 65, two on the front wall outside, two on the back, and four around the roof edge. Twelve torches total.
  8. Sleep. Right-click the bed as night falls. This sets your spawn point — the first claim in your new territory.

Success Checklist

  • [ ] Walls complete from Y 64 to Y 67 with no gaps
  • [ ] Door closes and opens
  • [ ] Four glass windows placed
  • [ ] Bed placed and slept in
  • [ ] Twelve torches burning
  • [ ] No hostile mobs inside the house at dawn

Missionary Note

A house is the first act of claiming. Before you can bring light to the world, you need a place to keep your own. The Old Builders forgot this, and their light went out. Build small, build solid, build light — then move outward. The world is taken back one foundation at a time.

MISSION 2 — THE BREAD BASKET

Difficulty: Easy

Mission Briefing

A missionary marches on bread. The wild grass around the camp carries wheat seeds — the Old Builders’ farms are gone, but their grain survives in the tall grass, waiting to be gathered and planted again. This mission turns wild seed into a working farm: the first food supply of the reclamation.

Objective

Build a fenced 9×9 wheat farm at the mission field, plant 32 wheat seeds, and harvest 32 wheat. One water source must water the whole field.

Supplies

Item Quantity
Wheat seeds 32
Hoe (wooden or stone) 1
Water bucket 1
Fence 28
Fence gate 1
Torches 8

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
Field corner (northwest) −45 63 80
Field corner (southeast) −37 63 88
Water channel −41 63 84
Fence perimeter −46 to −36 63 79 to 89
Gate −45 63 84

The field spans X −45 to −37 and Z 80 to 88. A single water source at (−41, 63, 84) sits in the middle, hydrating all 80 farmland blocks around it.

The Plan

  1. Collect the seeds. Break tall grass until you have 32 wheat seeds. (You may find more — keep them; you will need them.)
  2. Till the field. With the hoe, till every block in the 9×9 area except the center block at (−41, 63, 84).
  3. Water it. Use the water bucket at the center. The water will flow one block in every direction, hydrating the entire field. Cover the source with a slab if you want to walk across without falling in.
  4. Plant. Plant all 32 seeds in the tilled soil. If you have more seeds, plant them too — a full field is a richer harvest.
  5. Fence it. Ring the field with fence: 28 pieces, one block outside the tilled edge. Place the fence gate on the west side at (−45, 63, 84) so the field stays open to you and closed to mobs.
  6. Light the edges. Place 8 torches on the fence corners and midpoint posts so no mobs spawn on or near the field at night.
  7. Wait and harvest. Wheat grows in stages. When it is golden and tall, break it and collect the wheat. You need 32 in your inventory to complete the mission — but replant the seeds you drop. A bread basket is a cycle, not a one-time harvest.

Success Checklist

  • [ ] 32 wheat harvested and in inventory
  • [ ] Field fully fenced with a working gate
  • [ ] One water source hydrating the field
  • [ ] 8 torches placed around the perimeter
  • [ ] Seeds replanted for the next harvest

Missionary Note

The world will not feed you because you ask. It feeds you because you work. The Old Builders’ fields were overtaken by grass the moment they stopped tending them — that is the law of this world: whatever you do not tend, the wild takes back. A farm is a promise you renew every day.

MISSION 3 — THE LANTERN ROAD

Difficulty: Easy

Mission Briefing

The way from spawn to the eastern ridge is dark, and in the dark the world’s creatures hunt. The Old Builders believed roads were for carts. The missionary knows better: roads are for light. A lit road is a line drawn through the night, a claim staked in torchlight from horizon to horizon.

Objective

Light a torch path from spawn (0, 63, 0) to the eastern ridge at (100, 64, 100), placing 32 torches along the route. Then build a 10-block-high torch tower at the ridge and light its crown.

Supplies

Item Quantity
Coal 48
Sticks 48
Cobblestone 64
Stone slabs 32

(48 coal + 48 sticks = 48 torches. You will place 32 on the road, keep the rest for the tower and spares.)

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
Path start 0 63 0
Path end (ridge) 100 64 100
Torch spacing every 3 blocks along the diagonal — —
Tower base 100 64 100
Tower crown 100 74 100

The path runs diagonally northeast: X and Z rise together, 0 → 100. Place a torch every 4 blocks: (4, 63, 4), (8, 63, 8), (12, 63, 12), and so on until (100, 64, 100).

The Plan

  1. Gather fuel and light. Mine 48 coal. Craft 48 sticks, then 48 torches. (You will need more later in the book — this is your first torch run; it will not be your last.)
  2. Pave the stepping stones. Lay cobblestone and slabs in short stretches along the diagonal so the route is walkable. You do not need a full road — a missionary builds for light, not for carts.
  3. Plant the torches. Starting at (3, 63, 3), place a torch roughly every three blocks along the diagonal — about 32 torches over the run. Where the ground rises, put the torch on a cobblestone stub so it never sits below your eye line.
  4. Reach the ridge. The path ends at (100, 64, 100). From here you can see back to spawn — which is exactly the point.
  5. Build the tower. At (100, 64, 100), stack cobblestone to Y 74. Place a torch on top of the crown and four more around the sides at Y 73. This is the Lantern of the East: the first landmark of the reclamation.
  6. Check the gaps. Walk the path at night. No stretch of darkness may exceed 8 blocks. Add torches where the light pools thin.

Success Checklist

  • [ ] 32 torches placed along the path at roughly 4-block intervals
  • [ ] Path visible end to end at night from spawn
  • [ ] Tower at (100, 64, 100) standing 10 blocks high
  • [ ] Tower crown lit with 5 torches
  • [ ] No dark gap longer than 8 blocks anywhere on the route

Missionary Note

Light is the first weapon of the missionary. Mobs do not respect your sword until they fear your torches. Every torch you plant is a small sermon: this ground is claimed, this ground is watched, this ground belongs to the light again.

MISSION 4 — THE WATCHTOWER

Difficulty: Easy

Mission Briefing

To the northwest, the forest opens into a plain that the Old Builders called the Quiet Field. It is not quiet anymore — it is overtaken. From the rise at (−120, −40) you can see the whole plain, and something else: the first true test of a builder. A tower that can see is a tower that can warn. The reclamation needs eyes.

Objective

Build a 12-block-high watchtower at the mission site, with a working ladder, a fenced observation platform, and 16 torches. From the top, you must be able to see the Lantern of the East at (100, 64, 100).

Supplies

Cobblestone 192
Oak planks 96
Ladders 24
Fence 16
Glass 8
Torches 16

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
Tower footprint (southwest corner) −120 64 −40
Tower footprint (northeast corner) −114 64 −34

Wall tops −120 to −114 76 −40 to −34
Observation platform −119 to −115 76 −39 to −35
Ladder (north face) −117 64–75 −40
Windows −120 70 −37, and −117

The tower is a 7×7 hollow shaft. Its walls rise from Y 64 to Y 76 — 12 blocks of stone against the sky.

The Plan

  1. Gather stone. Mine 192 cobblestone. A nearby hill should cover most of it.
  2. Lay the base. Build the 7×7 footprint two blocks high (Y 64–65). Leave the inside hollow.
  3. Raise the shaft. From the base, raise the four walls to Y 76 — ten more blocks. Leave the ladder shaft open on the north face at (−117, Z −40).
  4. Windows. At Y 70, place glass at (−120, 70, −37) on the west wall and (−117, 70, −40) on the north wall. Small windows, watchful eyes.
  5. The ladder. Run ladders up the north face shaft from Y 64 to Y 75 — 24 ladders, no gaps. Test the climb.
  6. The platform. At Y 76, floor the top with oak planks. Ring it with fence. Place torches in the four corners and along the fence — 16 torches total on the tower.
  7. Look east. From the platform, face (100, 64, 100). If the night is clear, the Lantern of the East answers your light with its own.

Success Checklist

  • [ ] Tower stands 12 blocks from base to platform
  • [ ] Ladder climbs the full height without gaps
  • [ ] Platform floored and fully fenced
  • [ ] 16 torches placed
  • [ ] Lantern of the East visible from the top

Missionary Note

The Watchtower teaches the second law of the mission field: see before you are seen. The creatures of the overtaken world ambush what they can surprise. A tower does not fight for you — it sees for you, and seeing is half the battle. Raise your eyes, and the night loses its advantage.

MISSION 5 — THE FIRST DESCENT

Difficulty: Easy

Mission Briefing

The surface can feed you and shelter you, but it cannot arm you. Iron waits below — and below is where the world keeps its oldest secrets and its darkest rooms. Every missionary must learn to go down. This mission teaches the descent: a proper shaft, a proper ladder, a proper light discipline, and the first real ore of the reclamation.

Objective

Dig a 1×2 vertical shaft at the mission site down to Y 16, then branch-mine the tunnel at Y 16 and collect 32 iron ore.

Supplies

Item Quantity
Stone pickaxes 3
Ladders 40
Torches 32
Oak planks 32
Cooked food 16

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
Shaft mouth 50 63 −70
Shaft bottom 50 16 −70
Branch tunnel 50 → 70 16 −70
Side branches 50 → 70 16 −72 and −68

The shaft drops 47 blocks straight down from (50, 63, −70) to (50, 16, −70). At the bottom, a branch tunnel runs east from X 50 to X 70 at Y 16, with side branches at Z −72 and Z −68.

The Plan

  1. Prepare. Craft 3 stone pickaxes (48 cobblestone + 3 sticks × 3), 40 ladders, and 32 torches. Eat before you descend — mining is hungry work.
  2. Dig the shaft. At (50, 63, −70), dig a 1×2 hole straight down. Place ladders on one side of the shaft as you go, every block, no gaps. Torch the shaft every 8 blocks as you descend.
  3. Follow the floor. When your Y readout says 16, stop. You are at the mission floor — deep enough for iron in abundance, safe from lava lakes above the diamond layer.
  4. Branch-mine. Dig the main tunnel east from (50, 16, −70) to (70, 16, −70). Then dig side branches two blocks apart at Z −72 and Z −68, 20 blocks long each. This pattern exposes the most ore for the least digging.
  5. Collect the iron. Mine every iron ore block you find with your stone pickaxe. Do not smelt yet — this mission is about gathering. You need 32 blocks of iron ore in your inventory.
  6. Light as you go. Torch every tunnel every 8 blocks. A dark tunnel is a mob nursery. You are a missionary of light — even underground.
  7. Come back up. Climb the ladder. Check your Y as you rise: 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 — the world’s floors rolling past like stairs.

Success Checklist

  • [ ] Shaft bottom reached at Y 16
  • [ ] Ladders placed the full 47-block descent
  • [ ] Branch tunnels dug to spec
  • [ ] 32 iron ore in inventory
  • [ ] Every tunnel lit, no spawns in the shaft

Missionary Note

The descent is the third lesson: the world’s wealth is buried, and so is its danger. The Old Builders mined greedily and left their shafts dark; the creatures of the deep moved in behind them. When you go down, light everything and leave nothing behind. A clean mine is a claim that stays claimed.

TIER II — NORMAL

The middle five missions take you farther from spawn and deeper into the world. You will carve rooms out of the earth, run iron through fire, bridge a chasm, cross into the Nether, and make redstone obey. The easy days are over; the great work begins.

MISSION 6 — THE UNDERGROUND STRONGROOM

Difficulty: Normal

Mission Briefing

The surface is safe now — your house stands, your farm feeds you, your tower watches. But a missionary’s treasure should not sit where every wandering eye can see it. Two hundred blocks east and three hundred south, a hill hides a secret: the perfect place for a strongroom. You will cut a staircase into the hill and carve a home out of the earth’s bones.

Objective

Cut a staircase tunnel from the surface at (200, 63, 300) down to (200, 30, 300), then carve a 15×15×4 room around that point and furnish it with 16 chests, 8 furnaces, 4 crafting tables, and a bed.

Supplies

Ceiling 193–207 34 293–307

The staircase drops 33 blocks straight down the Z axis at X 200. At Y 30, the room opens: X 193–207, Z 293–307 — a 15×15 floor with a 4-block ceiling height.

The Plan

  1. Mark the mouth. At (200, 63, 300) on the hillside, dig a 2-wide entrance.
  2. Cut the staircase. Dig down in a straight run, 2 blocks wide, with a step down every block. Torch both walls every 8 blocks. Check your Y as you go: 55, 45, 35 — and stop at 30.

  4. Seal and light. Line the walls with torches on every pillar face — a 15×15 room needs about 40 torches to stay spawn-proof. Place the rest along the staircase.

  6. Doors. Place a door at the tunnel mouth and a second at the room entrance. The strongroom is yours; the world stays outside.
  7. Inventory. Fill the chests with everything you have gathered so far — iron ore from Mission 5, wheat from Mission 2, spare cobble and coal. A missionary who loses his stores loses his momentum.

  • [ ] 16 chests placed
  • [ ] 8 furnaces placed

  • [ ] Bed placed; no mob spawns anywhere in the room

Missionary Note

The strongroom is the fourth lesson: what you gather, you must guard. The Old Builders piled their wealth in the open and it was scattered in a single night. Bury what you love, light what you bury, and the world will have to work to take it from you.

MISSION 7 — THE IRONWORKS

Difficulty: Normal

Mission Briefing

Your chests are full of iron ore, but ore is only a promise. Iron becomes useful only when it passes through fire. Three hundred fifty blocks east of spawn, an outcrop of stone at (350, 64, 100) is the natural site for the Ironworks — a row of furnaces that will turn the promise of ore into the currency of the reclamation: iron ingots.

Objective

Build an 8-furnace smelting works at the mission site with a hopper-fed chest system, then smelt 64 iron ingots and bank them in the chest.

Supplies

Item Quantity
Iron ore 64 (mined, not smelted)
Cobblestone 128
Furnaces 8
Chests 4
Hoppers 8
Coal 64
Crafting table 1

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
Furnace row 350 → 357 64 100
Hopper row (below) 350 → 357 63 100
Collection chests 352–353 62 102–103

Eight furnaces sit in a row along Z 100, X 350 through 357. Each furnace feeds a hopper directly beneath it at Y 63; the hoppers feed chests at (352–353, 62, 102–103).

The Plan

  1. Feed the mission. Mine 64 iron ore if your stores are short — follow the Mission 5 branch-mine pattern in fresh ground. You also need 64 coal: any coal seam at Y 16–40 will do.
  2. Build the works. Place 8 furnaces in a row: (350, 64, 100) through (357, 64, 100).
  3. Plumb the hoppers. Dig out the blocks beneath each furnace (Y 63) and place a hopper there, pointed down. Dig a small channel at Y 62 leading to the chest position at (352–353, 62, 102–103). Place the four chests.
  4. Wire the flow. Each hopper must point toward the next until the line reaches the chests. Sneak-place the hoppers facing the correct direction — this is the fiddliest step, and the most rewarding.
  5. Fire the works. Load each furnace with 8 coal and 8 iron ore. Light them. As each ingot smelts, the hopper below catches it and carries it to the chest.
  6. Bank the ingots. Run the works until 64 iron ingots sit in the chest. Count them. When the count is right, the mission is done.

Success Checklist

  • [ ] 8 furnaces in a row at the mission coordinates
  • [ ] 8 hoppers connected, all feeding the chest line
  • [ ] 4 chests placed and reachable
  • [ ] 64 iron ingots banked in the chest
  • [ ] Works lit and safe to operate at night

Missionary Note

Ore is potential; ingot is power. The Ironworks teaches the fifth lesson: build systems, not just things. The Old Builders smelted one furnace at a time and spent their days tending fires. You will build a machine that tends itself while you do the next mission. Systems are how a single missionary reclaims a world.

MISSION 8 — THE CHASM BRIDGE

Difficulty: Normal

Mission Briefing

Four hundred blocks east of spawn, the land tears open. A ravine — the Old Builders called it the God-Split — runs for hundreds of blocks, darker than a wound, deeper than a well. The eastern lands beyond it hold the Nether gate site, but the ravine blocks the way. The creatures below own the dark. The missionary’s answer is not to go around. The missionary’s answer is to build across.

Objective

Bridge the ravine from the west lip at (400, 70, −200) to the east lip at (470, 70, −180), with a 2-wide plank deck, fences on both sides, and 16 torches. Cross it safely.

Supplies

Item Quantity
Oak planks 192
Fence 192
Torches 16
Cobblestone 64

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
West lip 400 70 −200
East lip 470 70 −180
Deck 400 → 470 70 −200 → −180
Torch spacing every 8 blocks along the deck — —

The ravine runs roughly southwest–northeast. The bridge crosses at Y 70, 7 blocks above the surrounding ground (Y 63). The deck runs about 70 blocks from X 400 to X 470, drifting south as it goes (Z −200 to −180).

The Plan

  1. Scout both lips. Walk the ravine edge at (400, 70, −200) and find the matching ledge at (470, 70, −180). Mark both with torches.
  2. Build the anchor. At the west lip, lay a cobblestone pad 4×4 so the bridge has a solid footing.
  3. Push the deck. Walk out from the pad, placing planks in pairs — two wide, one block of air on either side. Check the ravine floor below: it is a long way down. Do not look too long.
  4. Fence as you go. After every two plank pairs, place fence along both edges. It feels slow. It is what keeps you alive.
  5. Light the way. Every 8 blocks, a torch on the fence — 16 in all. The God-Split will glow like a river of fireflies.
  6. Reach the far pad. Build a matching cobblestone pad on the east lip at (470, 70, −180). The deck now spans lip to lip.
  7. Cross it. Walk the full length, west to east. Do not sprint. Do not jump. The bridge is yours; walk it like you own it.

Success Checklist

  • [ ] Deck 2 blocks wide, no gaps anywhere along the span
  • [ ] Fence on both sides for the full length
  • [ ] 16 torches placed at intervals
  • [ ] Anchor pads on both lips
  • [ ] Crossed the ravine safely, no falls, no deaths

Missionary Note

The bridge is the sixth lesson: obstacles are invitations. The Old Builders looked at the God-Split and saw the end of their road; they turned back, and their eastern claims were lost. The missionary looks at a chasm and sees a path that does not exist yet — and then builds it. Every block you place over the void is a refusal to be stopped.

MISSION 9 — THE GATE TO EMBER

Difficulty: Normal

Mission Briefing

Five hundred blocks east and four hundred south, the air changes. The trees thin, the grass yellows, and the ground feels hot underfoot. The Old Builders’ maps mark this place with a single word: EMBER. This is where the world’s second layer begins — the Nether, a dimension of fire and ash. The missionary does not fear it; the missionary gates into it, claims a foothold, and comes back with proof.

Objective

Build and light a Nether portal at the mission site, enter the Nether, build a 5×5×4 obsidian-and-cobble bunker at the Nether landing point, and return to the Overworld with 16 netherrack and 4 glowstone.

Supplies

Item Quantity
Obsidian 14
Flint and steel 1
Diamond pickaxe 1
Cobblestone 64
Torches 16
Cooked food 16
Iron sword 1

Coordinates

Landmark X Y Z
Portal frame (Overworld) 500 64 −400
Portal arrival (Nether) 62 64 −50
Nether bunker corner 60 64 −52
Nether bunker corner 64 64 −48

(Nether coordinates are roughly the Overworld’s divided by 8: 500 ÷ 8 ≈ 62, −400 ÷ 8 = −50.)

The Plan

  1. Gather obsidian. Find a lava pool and pour water over it — or mine the obsidian you find with the diamond pickaxe. You need 10 blocks.

Never fire two rockets in a row; the second one is always the one you will need to live. Land on the halfway rock at (−290, 64, −215). Count your rockets. Do not wake the gargoyles until you are ready.

If you miss, the golden apple and the healing potions are the price of carelessness. When the fourth anchor dies, the shadow knows. Six shadow-touched endermen rise from the island’s rim and walk toward you. They do not blink, and they do not teleport away. Do not fight them.

  8. Break the crown. The moment the fourth anchor falls, drink the splash potion of Slow Falling, fire one rocket straight up, and sail out over the pit. The crown burns below you, unwarded at last. From the air, draw the bow and put one arrow into its heart. It does not detonate like the anchors — it splits, screams, and dies, guttering out over the void like a snuffed candle. The rift dies with it, and the sky above your world stays closed. Then turn west and fire a rocket before the endermen’s reach finds you. Do not look back. You have unlocked a tomb.

  9. Fly the long way home. Home is not south; it is one flight to the halfway rock, one flight to the city, one bridge to the main island, one walk to the door. Boost, glide, count. At (−290, 64, −215), land, rest, eat the last cooked beef, and count again. From the halfway rock to the city is one more flight — land on the ship’s rail or the tower’s roof at (460, 90, 380). Reclaim your 40-block bridge across the narrows, walk the main island, and ignore the endermen that watch from the dark. Put torches where your feet need them. You are almost home.

  10. Pass the door. Two shadow-touched endermen stand guard at the exit portal at (0, 64, 0) — the shadow posted them at the only door home. Kill them with the netherite sword, shield up, or sprint past them; the portal is three blocks away. Step through. The End closes behind you like a held breath, and you emerge in the stronghold library at (812, 34, 213). Look up through the crack in the ceiling. The sky above spawn is clear. The moon has not yet filled. You brought the night home empty.

The shadow built its last court out of nothing — no stone to tunnel, no burrow to hide in, no floor to stand on. It thought a court of empty air would be the one thing you could not build upon. MISSION 20 — THE CHOIR THAT FELL

Difficulty: Expert

Mission Briefing

The Void Crown is broken. The moon filled and found an empty sky, and for one night the world above the stone was entirely yours. The shadow did not die with the crown — it descended, as it always does, into its oldest reserve. It went east, past the dark forest edge, to a mansion in a clearing that no map in your strongroom remembers: the Hollow House at (2100, 63, 1450), where a thousand years ago the Old Builders caged their four light-singers. The allays. The first songs of the making of the world, kept in iron bars instead of open air.

The mansion is ruled by the Hollow Court — a ravager at the gate, evokers in the wings, axemen in the halls, and at the heart of it all the Hollow Monarch, wearing a totem of undying smeared black with everything the shadow has fed it. The court has not been drinking the allays’ song for pleasure. It has been recording it. The totem is that recording, sung backwards. At the second sunset from now, the Monarch means to let it play — and when the backwards song reaches its last note, the Great Raid kindles: nine waves, nine fronts, every reclaimed site struck in the same hour. Your home at (24, 64, 12). Your farm at (−45, 63, 80). Your tower, your bridge, your ironworks, your sky city, and the Beacon itself. The dark has never once attacked all of it at once. It has learned the cost of attacking any of it.

The clock is the shape of this mission. The second sunset is the kindling hour, and the shadow has shut the roads that would cheat it — the old Nether gate at (500, 64, −400) is watched by patrols, and the bunker at (62, 64, −50) echoes with footsteps that are not yours. Your road is the long road: 2,100 blocks overland, east and south, into a dark forest that grows no kinder at dusk.

And one more sorrow, missionary. The night the Void Crown fell, the shadow’s raiders emptied the strongroom at (200, 30, 300). No netherite. No rockets. No soft armor. You will go in iron: one sword, one shield, eight arrows, one golden apple. Count them twice. The mansion will arm you from its own dead — every evoker in the court drops its totem, every axeman may leave his axe — but the first hour is fought bare. The shadow has read every mission you have fought, found your armory, and closed your shortcuts. It has built the one court it thinks you cannot win. Expert means you walk into that court anyway, and you take the song back.

Missionary Note

The shadow never understood the allays. It heard their song and thought it was fuel — something to be drunk, recorded, played backwards, spent. It is the oldest error in the world, and you have now profited from it twice in one mission: it emptied your armory and thought you would come bare, and it caged the light and thought the light would stay.

You went into the hardest house the Overworld owns with an iron sword and eight arrows, and the house fed you everything else. That is the missionary’s arithmetic: scarcity is not weakness, it is honesty. It tells you exactly what you are — and what you are, it turns out, is enough. A shield, a sword, a count you kept, a song you refused to let die. The shadow gave you the hardest test it could build, and the test broke because a broken test does not change what the missionaries do.

And the allays follow you now. Not because you are their keeper — you are not. They follow you because you broke the cage, put a small light in each of their hands, and carried them to a bigger one. That is the whole shape of this work, missionary. Every mission is the same four steps: find what the dark has caged, break the bars, give it a light to hold, and walk home together where the bigger light waits.
